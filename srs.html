1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document defines the functional and non‑functional requirements for the EronixTech Multi‑Vendor ERP Platform. The system is designed to provide a comprehensive business management solution that allows multiple vendors to manage their inventory, invoicing, billing, purchasing, reporting, and financial tracking within a single platform. The system operates on a subscription‑based model with a centralized Super Admin controlling the entire ecosystem.

1.2 Scope

EronixTech Multi‑Vendor ERP is a SaaS‑based enterprise resource planning system targeted at:

  • Retail Businesses
  • Wholesalers
  • Distributors
  • Manufacturers
  • Service Providers
  • E‑commerce Sellers

The system will help businesses:

  • Manage products and inventory across multiple warehouses
  • Generate invoices and bills
  • Track customer and vendor transactions
  • Monitor stock movement in real‑time
  • Generate comprehensive financial reports
  • Automate notifications and reminders
  • Improve operational efficiency
  • Scale business operations seamlessly

1.3 Objectives

Multi‑Tenancy Support multiple vendors with complete data isolation and white‑label capabilities.
Real‑Time Inventory Maintain accurate stock levels across warehouses with instant sync.
Financial Visibility Real‑time sales, purchase, profit reports with multi‑currency & tax support.
Scalability From small startups to large enterprises with elastic infrastructure.
Compliance GST, , and audit‑ready reporting.
Operational Efficiency Streamline processes across departments with automation.

3. User Roles & Permissions

3.1 Super Admin

The Super Admin controls the entire multi‑vendor platform.

Responsibilities: Manage all vendors/tenants, create/assign subscription plans, configure system‑wide settings, monitor platform performance, view all vendor analytics, manage platform security, handle disputes, backups, platform monetization.

3.2 Vendor Admin

The primary administrator for an individual vendor/tenant.

Permissions: Full CRUD across all vendor modules including Dashboard, Product Management, Inventory, Customer, Vendor, Billing, Invoice, Purchase, Payments, Reports, Notifications, User Management, Document, Audit, Expense, Support, Tax, Financial Period, Warehouse.

3.3 Vendor Accountant

Handles financial transactions and reporting within vendor.

Permissions: Financial Dashboard, Billing (Create/Edit), Invoice Management, Payments & Receivables (Full), Financial Reports, Expense (Full), Tax (Full), Financial Period (View/Edit), Customer/Vendor (View Only).

3.4 Vendor Inventory Manager

Responsible for stock operations.

Permissions: Product (Full), Inventory (Full), Purchase (Create/Receive), Warehouse (Full), Inventory Reports, Billing/Vendor (View Only).

3.5 Vendor Sales Executive

Manages customer sales activities.

Permissions: Customer (Full), Billing (Create/Edit), Invoice (Create/Edit), Sales Dashboard, Product/Inventory (View Only), Sales Reports.

3.6 Vendor Support Executive

Manages customer support tickets.

Permissions: Support & Ticket (Full), Customer (View Only), Knowledge Base (Full), Support Dashboard.

3.7 Customer Portal User

Customer self‑service access to vendor portal.

Permissions: Customer Portal (View), Invoice (View Own), Payments (View Own), Support (Create/View Own), Profile (Edit Own).

3.8 Dynamic RBAC Features

  • Custom role creation (Super Admin & Vendor Admin)
  • Fine‑grained permission assignment
  • Permission inheritance & role cloning

Permission Types: Module Access, Feature Access, Data Access (Own/Department/All), Field Access (Read/Write), Action Access (CRUD/Export/Import).

  • Vendor Onboarding: Guided setup wizard, configuration templates, sample data import.

  • Quick Widgets: Recent Invoices, Recent Payments, Pending Orders, Low Stock Alerts, Expiring Products, Upcoming Receivables, Recent Activities.

4.3 Product Management

Product Master

  • Product Information: Name, SKU (auto/custom), Barcode (EAN‑13/UPC), QR Code, Category (multi‑level), Brand, Unit of Measure, Description, HSN/SAC Code, Country of Origin, Manufacturing/Expiry Date, Warranty Period, Weight, Dimensions.
  • Pricing: Purchase Price, Selling Price (multiple tiers), Wholesale Price, Retail Price, Tax Rate, Discount Rules, Price History, Competitor Price Tracking, Markup/Margin, Minimum Selling Price.
  • Inventory: Opening Stock, Current Stock, Reorder Level, Min/Max Stock, Preferred/Alternative Suppliers, Reorder Quantity, Lead Time, Storage Location, Shelf Life.

Product Variants

  • Types: Color, Size, Weight, Capacity, Material, Style, Custom attributes.
  • Management: Variant combinations, individual SKU, variant‑specific pricing/inventory/images, bulk creation.

Product Tracking

  • Batch Tracking: Batch number, Manufacturing/Expiry date, batch quantity, quality check, location.
  • Serial Number Tracking: Unique serials, assignment history, warranty tracking.

Media, Import/Export, Bulk Operations

  • Media: Multiple images, videos, documents (manuals, certificates), alt text, gallery.
  • Import: CSV, Excel, JSON, XML with field mapping, validation, error reporting, duplicate handling.
  • Export: Excel, PDF, CSV, JSON, custom field selection, filtered/scheduled exports.
  • Bulk: Price update, category update, supplier update, delete, status change, tax update, SKU generation, image upload, tag assignment.

4.4 Customer Management

Customer Profile

  • Personal: Name, Mobile, Email, DOB, Gender, Type (Individual/Business).
  • Business: Company Name, GST, PAN, Business Type, Industry, Website, Annual Turnover.
  • Contact: Billing/Shipping Addresses (multiple), Contact Person, Designation, Office Phone, Mobile, Fax, Preferred Contact.
  • Financial: Credit Limit/Period, Payment Terms, Discount Percentage, Tax Exemption, Bank Details, UPI ID.

Customer History & Portal

  • History: Sales History, Payment History, Outstanding Balances, Credit/Debit Notes, Interaction History, Support Tickets, Communication History.
  • Analytics: Purchase Frequency, Average Order Value, Customer Lifetime Value, Last Purchase Date, Segment, RFM Analysis.
  • Portal: Login/Register, View/Download Invoices, Payment History, Outstanding Balance, Raise Support Tickets, Update Profile, Change Password, Order Status.

4.5 Vendor Management (Within Vendor)

  • Profile: Vendor Name, Contact Person, Mobile, Email, GST, PAN, Business Type, Registration Number, Category, Addresses, Phone, Website, Social Media, Bank Details, Payment Terms, Credit Period/Limit.
  • Records: Purchase Orders, Vendor Bills, Payment History, Outstanding Dues, Purchase Returns, Quality Ratings, Delivery Performance, Compliance Documents.

4.6 Purchase Management

  • Purchase Orders: Create, Approve, Reject, Receive Goods, Bulk PO creation, templates, status tracking. Types: Standard, Blanket, Contract, Emergency.
  • Purchase Processing: Goods Receipt Note (quality check, quantity verification, damaged goods handling, partial receipts), Vendor Bills (generate from received goods, bill verification/approval/payment), Purchase Returns (damaged/incorrect goods, return form, credit note).

4.7 Payments & Receivables

  • Receivables: Outstanding Invoices, Due Dates, Aging Reports (30/60/90+ days), Expected Payments, Collection Follow‑up, Reminder Automation, Payment Received, Partial Payments.
  • Payables: Vendor Bills, Vendor Payments, Due Dates, Payment Scheduling, Cash Flow Forecast, Payment Approvals, Bulk Payments.
  • Payment Processing: Incoming/Outgoing Payments, Journal Entries. Modes: Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS), Cheque, Cash, Online Payment, UPI, Pay Later, Credit Card.

4.8 Reports & Analytics

  • Sales Reports: Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Annual Sales, Sales by Product Category/Customer/Salesperson/Region/Payment Method, Sales Tax Report, GST Sales Report.
  • Inventory Reports: Stock Summary, Inventory Valuation, Dead Stock Report, Reorder Report, Inventory Turnover, Stock Aging, Warehouse‑wise/Batch‑wise/Serial‑wise Stock.
  • Financial Reports: Profit & Loss, Revenue Report, Expense Report, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, Budget vs Actual, Financial Ratios, Income Statement, Statement of Accounts.
  • Vendor Reports: Vendor Spend Analysis, Vendor Performance, Vendor Compliance, Vendor Payment History, Purchase Summary.
  • Custom Reports: Report builder, custom fields, scheduling, email reports, sharing, export (PDF, Excel, CSV).

4.9 Notification System

  • Channels: Email, SMS, WhatsApp, In‑app Notifications, Telegram.
  • Events: Sales (New Order, Status Update, Payment Received, Invoice Due/Overdue), Inventory (Expiry, Low Stock, Adjustment, Batch Expiry), Purchase (PO Created/Approved, Goods Received, Vendor Bill Due), Customer (Registration, Portal Login, Support Ticket).
  • Scheduling: Immediate, Scheduled, Recurring (Daily/Weekly/Monthly), Batch processing.

4.10 User & Role Management

  • User Management: Create, Edit, Suspend, Delete, Bulk Import, User Groups, User Status, User Profile (Full Name, Email, Mobile, Role, Department, Employee ID, Profile Picture, Signature).
  • Dynamic RBAC: Role creation/editing/deletion, permission assignment, inheritance, role cloning, role hierarchy. Permission Types: Module Access, Feature Access, Data Access (Own/Department/All), Field Access (Read/Write), Action Access (CRUD/Export/Import).
  • Security: Session Management, Login History, Password Policies, Social Login (Google).
  • Audit Trail: User Login, User Actions, Data Changes, System Events.

4.11 Document Management

  • Supported: Invoices, Purchase Orders, Bills, Contracts, Attachments, Employee Documents, Compliance Documents, Insurance, Legal.
  • Operations: Upload, Download, Search, Archive, Bulk Upload, Preview, Sharing, Version Control.
  • Categories: Sales, Purchase, Financial, Legal, Compliance, Employee, Customer, Vendor.
  • Security: Role‑based access, document expiry, encryption, watermarking, view/download logs.

  • Ticket Types: General Inquiry, Complaint, Feature Request, Support Request, Bug Report, Technical Issue, Billing Issue.

5. Billing System (Deep Dive)

Overview: The Billing System is the financial engine of EronixTech ERP, enabling businesses to process sales, manage payments, and handle complex financial operations with ease. It supports both Point-of-Sale (POS) and advanced billing scenarios.

5.1 POS Billing

Features:

  • Product Search: Quick lookup by Name, SKU, or Barcode scanning.
  • Quick Billing: One-click billing for fast-paced retail environments.
  • Customer Lookup: Search and attach customers to transactions.
  • Cart Management: Add, remove, and update quantities in real-time.
  • Discount Application: Apply line-item or invoice-level discounts.
  • Tax Calculation: Automatic GST/CGST/SGST/IGST calculation.
  • Payment Processing: Multiple payment methods with split payments.

5.2 Payment Methods

Support for diverse payment options:

Cash – Physical currency
Credit/Debit Card – Swipe/EMI
UPI – Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.
Bank Transfer – NEFT/RTGS/IMPS
Cheque – With clearance tracking
Buy Now Pay Later – Installment plans

5.3 Advanced Billing Features

5.3.1 Discounts & Promotions

  • Line Item Discount: Percentage or flat amount per product.
  • Invoice Discount: Overall discount on the total bill.
  • Coupon Discount: Code-based promotional discounts.
  • Volume Discount: Tiered pricing based on quantity.
  • Seasonal Discount: Time-based promotional offers.
  • Bundle Discount: Discounts on product combinations.

5.3.2 Installment Plans (EMI)

  • EMI Calculation: Automatic interest and tenure calculation.
  • Payment Schedule: Generate and track installment schedules.
  • Down Payment: Partial upfront payment with balance in installments.
  • Balance Payment: Track remaining balances across installments.

5.3.4 Taxation

  • Automatic Tax Calculation: Based on product, customer, and location.
  • GST (CGST/SGST/IGST): Full GST compliance for India.
  • Tax Exemptions: Handle tax-exempt customers/products.
  • Tax Inclusive/Exclusive: Support both pricing models.
  • E‑Invoicing: Generate tax-compliant e-invoices.
  • Tax Reports: GST/ /Sales Tax summary reports.

6. Invoice Management (Deep Dive)

Overview: Invoice Management powers the entire billing lifecycle—from creation to payment and reconciliation. It supports multiple invoice types, custom templates, automated workflows, and comprehensive tracking.

6.1 Invoice Types

Sales Invoice – Standard customer billing
Purchase Invoice – Vendor billing
Tax Invoice – GST/ compliant
Proforma Invoice – Estimate/quotation
Credit Note – Refund/adjustment
Debit Note – Additional charge
Recurring Invoice – Subscription billing
Consolidated Invoice – Multiple orders

6.2 Invoice Features

6.2.1 Auto Number Generation

  • Format Customization: INV-{YYYY}-{XXXXXX}, INV-{VENDOR}-{SEQ}
  • Vendor Prefix: Each vendor can have a unique prefix.
  • Counter Management: Sequential numbering with year/period reset.
  • Manual Override: Admin can set custom numbers when needed.

6.2.2 Invoice Templates

  • Custom Branding: Logo, Colors, Fonts, Header/Footer.
  • Template Preview: See changes in real-time.
  • Variable Placeholders: Dynamic content (customer name, amount, etc.).

6.2.3 Invoice Actions

  • Create: Generate new invoice from scratch or from order.
  • Edit: Modify invoice details (with audit trail).
  • Preview: View before sending/printing.
  • Print: Physical print with print-optimized layout.
  • Download PDF: High-quality PDF generation.
  • Email: Send directly to customer with attachments.
  • WhatsApp: Instant sharing via WhatsApp Business API.
  • SMS: Short notification with download link.
  • QR Code: Payment QR code on invoice.
  • Share Link: Secure public link for payment.

6.3 Recurring Invoices

  • Frequency Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-annual, Yearly, Custom.
  • Auto‑Send: Automatically send invoices on schedule.
  • Auto‑Payment: Charge saved payment method automatically.
  • Recurring Items: Fixed products/services per cycle.

  • Price Update: Change pricing for future cycles.
  • Customer Notification: Alert before and after billing.

7. Inventory Management (Deep Dive)

Overview: Inventory Management is the operational backbone of EronixTech ERP, providing real-time visibility into stock levels, warehouse operations, and material movement. It ensures accurate stock tracking, reduces wastage, and optimizes supply chain efficiency.

7.1 Stock Operations

7.1.1 Stock In (Increases Inventory)

  • Purchase Orders: Receive goods from suppliers.
  • Purchase Returns: Returned goods from customers.
  • Manual Adjustments: Positive stock corrections.
  • Manufacturing: Finished goods from production.
  • Transfer In: Stock received from other warehouses.
  • Opening Stock: Initial inventory setup.

7.1.2 Stock Out (Decreases Inventory)

  • Sales Orders: Goods sold to customers.
  • Sales Returns: Goods returned by customers.
  • Damaged/Expired Goods: Write-off due to damage or expiry.
  • Transfers Out: Stock moved to other warehouses.
  • Production Consumption: Raw materials used in manufacturing.
  • Wastage: Operational wastage/loss.
  • Samples: Free samples given to customers.

7.2 Stock Adjustments

  • Positive Adjustment: Add stock (found/locate).
  • Negative Adjustment: Remove stock (theft/loss/damage).
  • Damage Write‑off: Stock damaged beyond use.
  • Expiry Write‑off: Stock expired and unusable.
  • Workflow: Create request → Approval → Execution → Audit.

7.3 Stock Monitoring & Alerts

Low Stock Alert – Reorder level reached
Overstock Alert – Max stock exceeded
Dead Stock Alert – No movement for X days
Expiry Alert – Product nearing expiry
Reorder Alert – Suggested reorder
Quality Alert – Quality check due

Real‑time Stock Status: Current Stock, Reserved Stock, Available Stock, In Transit, At Warehouse.

7.4 Inventory Reports

  • Stock Ledger: Detailed transaction history per product.
  • Stock Movement Report: Summary of all stock movements.
  • Batch‑wise Inventory: Stock by batch numbers.
  • Serial‑wise Inventory: Track individual serialized items.
  • Inventory Turnover: Sales vs. average stock ratio.
  • Stock Valuation: Current stock value (FIFO/LIFO/Weighted Average).
  • Aging Analysis: Stock by age (days in inventory).
  • Slow/Fast‑moving: Identify sales velocity.
  • Warehouse‑wise: Stock per warehouse.
  • Category‑wise: Stock by product category.
  • Brand‑wise: Stock by brand.

7.5 Warehouse Management

  • Multi‑Warehouse Support: Manage multiple physical locations.
  • Zones & Racks: Organize warehouses into zones, racks, and bins.
  • Location Mapping: QR/Barcode-based location tracking.
  • Transfer Orders: Move stock between warehouses.
  • Warehouse Capacity: Track utilization and capacity.
  • Warehouse‑wise Stock: Real-time stock per warehouse.
  • Operations: Create, edit, delete, assign managers, set priority.

7.6 Inventory Audit & Reconciliation

  • Physical Count: Schedule and perform physical inventory counts.
  • Cycle Counting: Regular partial counts for high-value items.
  • Reconciliation: Match system stock with physical count.
  • Variance Reporting: Identify and report discrepancies.
  • Adjustment Logs: All adjustments are logged with user and timestamp.
